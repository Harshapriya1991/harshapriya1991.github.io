Give Way: Roundabout Game

Find your gap. Take your exit. Don't hit anything.

Download on the App Store

How it works

  • Hold to drive, let go to brake
  • Give way to traffic from the right and wait for a gap
  • Each junction picks your exit: 1st, 2nd or 3rd
  • Signal properly, or take the wrong road

Endless levels

  • Busy roundabouts with up to six arms
  • Tight mini roundabouts and multi-lane spirals
  • Traffic-light roundabouts
  • Buses, lorries, cyclists, breakdowns and roadworks
  • Rain, night driving, and drivers who signal left and then don't turn

Garage and daily challenge

Earn points at every junction and spend them on 11 vehicles and 8 paint jobs. Come back every day for a new set of five junctions and build your streak.

Give Way is free to play and supported by ads. It's a game, not an official driving or theory test resource.
